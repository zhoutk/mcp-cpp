## Responsibilities

- Triage issues within 2 business days (Tier-1 commitment) using the standardized
  labels: type (`bug` / `enhancement` / `question`), status (`needs confirmation`,
  `needs repro`, `ready for work`, `good first issue`, `help wanted`), and priority
  (`P0`–`P3`).
- Resolve critical (P0) bugs within 7 days.
- Keep the conformance job green on `main`.
- Review pull requests and cut releases.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contributions — especially protocol work and conformance
fixes — are the path. Ask, or be invited.
